The final round is a team effort now.

Once someone calls Vinto, the other three are a coalition, and they can talk at the table: say what you hold, propose a move to a teammate, and build one shared plan, lane by lane. Anyone in the coalition can edit any part of it; the caller cannot see it. Watch the plan run on the felt before you commit, and read what it is worth against the caller before the cards turn over.

Bots are honest about what they know: they throw in only cards they have actually seen, and they keep a drawn Joker rather than putting it on the pile.

If online play asks you to update, the room has moved on to a newer version of the game; the button takes you to the App Store.

Thank you for playing.
