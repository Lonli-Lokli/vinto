Vinto's first release.

Five cards, two you have seen, and three opponents who are working out theirs at the same time. Call Vinto when you think you are lowest — be right and it pays, be wrong and it costs you.

Play solo against the computer at three strengths, fully offline. Or open a room, send six characters, and whoever gets them is at the table — no account and no sign-up, on a phone or in a browser.

There is a tutorial that deals you a real round rather than reading you the rules, and you can skip it.

Thank you for playing.
