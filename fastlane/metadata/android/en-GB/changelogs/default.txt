Vinto's first release.

Five cards, two you have seen, and three opponents working out theirs at the same time. Call Vinto when you think you are lowest. Being right pays, and being wrong costs you.

Solo against the computer at three strengths, fully offline. Or open a room, send six characters, and whoever gets them is at the table. No account, no sign-up.

There is a tutorial that deals a real round instead of reciting rules, and you can skip it.
